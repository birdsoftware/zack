How to Play

Mission

Choose an alien race, collect every reactor core, then fly into the glowing warp gate. Every 5 levels rescues a friend.

Pilots

Pick one of three alien commanders at the start. Click the avatar in the top bar any time to inspect the puzzle map.

Controls

  • Move with arrow keys or WASD.
  • On phones, drag the floating joystick to steer.
  • Hold Space to shoot.
  • On phones, hold the floating Shoot button.
  • Hold Shift to boost, or keep moving for auto boost.
  • Press P to pause, R to reset, and M to mute.

Blaster

Shots can destroy sentries and break most interior maze walls. Each level has one rainbow crystal that starts 10 seconds of auto-fire and auto-boost. Ancient blocks never break, crystal blocks take 5-7 hits, and some broken blocks leave sticky glue, rare black holes, or repair bots that patch two nearby blocks.

Upgrades

Each reactor core gives two ship upgrades. Beating a level gives two pilot upgrades for your main character.

Puzzle Map

Every beaten level reveals one map piece on a new planet map. Rescue 5 friends per race, for 75 total missions, and use numbered memory buttons to revisit completed puzzles.

Bosses

After each full puzzle, bosses get tougher. After 2 rescued friends, missions can have two bosses; after 4 friends, the final rescue can have three. Some fire homing rockets, worm bosses lash through maze paths, and the Venom Hydra can do both.

Survival

You start with 5 lives. If a sentry hits your ship, you respawn at the level start with a short shield.

Audio

Launch starts the background song. The Audio button opens music and effects sliders.
